## Enquiries
Because FormSubmit submitted requests were not delivered to the business inbox, it is **disabled** until separately verified. The form now opens a Gmail web compose draft, prefilled with visitor information. Visitors have to click Send in Gmail; the website itself does not submit or store personal information. Alternative actions:
- **Open Gmail** web compose directly, no native mail app required
- **Copy Email Address** via clipboard with a fallback for older browsers
- Visible, selectable text email address
- Click-to-call phone number

Do not reintroduce mailto/native email app links or automatic form claims until a verified form solution is configured.
