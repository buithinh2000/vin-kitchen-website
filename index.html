FAQ

Common questions before you start.

Which suburbs do you service?

We welcome enquiries from Bendigo, Echuca, Moama, Shepparton and surrounding towns. Travel, project scope and availability are confirmed individually.

How long does cabinet installation take?

Timeframes vary with the cabinet layout, manufacturing and site readiness. We discuss an installation schedule after confirming the project scope.

Can I replace just my kitchen doors?

Yes, where existing cabinets are suitable, replacement doors and visible panels can be a practical alternative to a full kitchen replacement.

Do you install stone benchtops?

Stone benchtops are supplied and installed separately by specialist partners. VIN Kitchen focuses on cabinetry.

What is included in a cabinetry quote?

Each quote will identify doors, carcasses, panels, hardware, delivery and fitting included in the project. Stone benchtops, plumbing, electrical work, appliances and other trades are separate unless expressly included.

Can you help with flat-pack cabinets?

Ask us about cabinet assembly and installation. Scope and scheduling are subject to availability and the supplied cabinet system.

How do I request a quote?

Start with your location, approximate dimensions, photos and the type of cabinetry you need. We can then discuss the next steps.

Options & inclusions

What can we help you choose?

We quote cabinetry based on your measurements, layout and selections. No fixed online price is promised without a project review.

Doors & Panels

Flat-panel or shaker profiles, colour and finish selection, visible end panels, fillers and kickboards.

Cabinet Hardware

Hinges, drawer runners, handles, soft-close options, pantry accessories and practical storage.

Installation Scope

Supply and fit cabinetry as quoted. Stone benchtops, electrical, plumbing, appliances and demolition are excluded unless separately agreed with an appropriate trade.
