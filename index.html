FAQ

Good to know.

Can I replace just my kitchen doors?

Yes, where existing cabinets are suitable, replacement doors and visible panels can be a practical alternative to a full kitchen replacement.

Do you install stone benchtops?

Stone benchtops are supplied and installed separately by specialist partners. VIN Kitchen focuses on cabinetry.

How do I request a quote?

Start with your location, approximate dimensions, photos and the type of cabinetry you need. We can then discuss the next steps.
